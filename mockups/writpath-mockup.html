Mockup · example data
Writ Planner"My writs" orders the writs you have; "Buy writs" finds the cheapest and the fastest set for a rep target from your Auctionator prices, one writ per customer per day. The line top right says how old the prices are.
Guided routeA step list with an arrow: flight paths you know, zone crossings and boats, then each customer. Its footer shows the running plan's cost, pouches so far and net. Tick turn-in steps to watch it change.
Writ shoppingAt the auction house, "Writ shopping" buys the plan's list one click at a time (the game needs a click per purchase), with a 25% price cap. It only opens while a plan has something to buy.
ClearThe Clear button in the planner and the red X in the guide header clear the route, the plan and its shopping list, after asking. What WritPath has learned stays.
